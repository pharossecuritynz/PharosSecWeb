# Discovery Questionnaire

*Sent to clients ahead of a Cyber Risk Review (or adapted for other services) to make the discovery session more efficient. Client can answer informally — this isn't a formal audit form.*

---

**Business name:**
**Completed by (name / role):**
**Date:**

## About the business

1. What does the business do, in a sentence or two?
2. Roughly how many staff, and how many locations (including remote/home-based staff)?
3. Do you have an outsourced IT provider or MSP? If yes, who, and what do they cover?
4. Is there anyone internally responsible for IT or security (even informally)?

## Systems and data

5. What are the main software systems/platforms the business relies on day to day? (e.g. Microsoft 365, Xero, an industry-specific system, a CRM)
6. Do you store customer or client personal information? Roughly how sensitive is it (e.g. basic contact details vs. financial/health information)?
7. Do you process payments or handle significant financial transactions online?
8. Do staff use personal devices for work, or work from personal networks (home/public wifi)?

## Current practices

9. Do staff use multi-factor authentication (MFA) — e.g. a code or app prompt in addition to a password — for email or other key systems?
10. How are passwords managed across the business (e.g. individually, a shared password manager, no formal approach)?
11. Do you have any written security policies currently? If yes, what do they cover?
12. How are backups handled for your critical data/systems?
13. Have you (or anyone you know in a similar business) experienced a phishing attempt, fraud attempt, or security incident in the past 2 years? What happened?

## Risk awareness

14. What's your biggest worry, if any, when it comes to cyber security for this business?
15. What prompted you to reach out to Pharos Security now?
16. Has a customer, insurer, or partner ever asked about your security practices? What did they ask?

## Wrap-up

17. Is there anything else about the business's systems, practices, or concerns that would be useful to know before we talk?

---

*This questionnaire is a discovery aid, not a formal audit — approximate or "I'm not sure" answers are completely fine. We'll clarify anything important in the discovery session itself.*
