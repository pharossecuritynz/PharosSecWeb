# Security Policy and Checklist Starter Pack — Index

*Structure for the [Security Policy and Checklist Starter Pack](../03_Services/security-policy-checklist-starter-pack.md) service. This file is the index/contents plan — each policy is drafted as its own short document (typically 1–3 pages each) using this outline.*

---

## Standard document set (confirm which apply during scoping)

### 1. Acceptable Use Policy
- Purpose and scope
- Acceptable use of business systems, devices, and internet access
- Personal use guidance (what's reasonable)
- Prohibited activities
- Consequences of non-compliance (kept brief; defer to HR policy for specifics)

### 2. Password and Authentication Policy
- Password requirements (or password manager mandate, preferred over complex rules)
- Multi-factor authentication requirement
- What to do if a password may be compromised

### 3. Device and Remote Working Policy
- Approved devices for work (company-issued vs. personal/BYOD)
- Requirements for personal device use (screen lock, updates, no jailbreaking)
- Safe remote/public wifi practices
- Lost or stolen device process

### 4. Data Handling Basics
- What counts as sensitive information at this business (tailored per client)
- Basic handling rules: where data can/can't be stored, sharing guidelines
- Retention and disposal basics

### 5. Incident Reporting Policy
- What counts as something to report (err on the side of "when in doubt, report it")
- Who to report to and how
- Explicit statement of blame-free culture
- What happens after a report is made

### 6. One-page Security Checklist (summary)
- A single-page distillation of the key points from all documents above, suitable for printing/pinning up or including in onboarding packs

## Tailoring notes (per client)

- Reference the client's actual tools/platforms by name (not generic placeholders) in the final delivered documents
- Reflect any genuinely existing practices already in place, rather than only introducing new rules
- Keep each document to 1–3 pages — length undermines the "plain-English, will actually be read" goal

## Delivery format

- Word document (editable, client can maintain going forward) plus PDF (for immediate distribution)
- Client's logo/branding applied if provided

## Rollout recommendation to include with delivery

> These policies are only useful if your team actually knows about them. We recommend introducing them via a short team meeting or alongside a Staff Cyber Awareness Session, rather than just emailing them out to be filed away.
