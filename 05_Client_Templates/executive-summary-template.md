# Executive Summary Template

*A reusable structure for the opening page of any client report. Keep to one page. Written for the business owner, not the technical reader.*

---

## Executive Summary

**Prepared for:** [Client Name]
**Prepared by:** Pharos Security
**Date:** [Date]
**Engagement:** [Service name, e.g. Cyber Risk Review]

### What we looked at

[1–2 sentences describing the scope in plain English — e.g. "We reviewed how [Client Name] manages access to systems, protects email and data, and prepares for potential incidents, based on a discovery session and review of current practices."]

### The overall picture

[2–4 sentences giving an honest, calm overview. Always acknowledge genuine strengths before gaps.
