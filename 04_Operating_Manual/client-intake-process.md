# Client Intake Process (SOP)

Covers everything from first contact to booking a discovery call.

## Trigger

A prospective client contacts Pharos Security via: website contact form, email, LinkedIn message, phone, or referral introduction.

## Steps

### 1. Acknowledge promptly
Respond within **1 business day** (aim for same-day). A short, warm acknowledgement — not a full answer yet.

> Template: see [../06_Sales_and_Marketing/follow-up-email-template.md](../06_Sales_and_Marketing/follow-up-email-template.md) for tone reference.

### 2. Do a 2-minute fit check
Before booking a call, sanity-check:
- Is this a New Zealand SME roughly in the 5–100 staff range? (See [../02_Business_Strategy/target-market.md](../02_Business_Strategy/target-market.md).)
- Is the request within [service boundaries](../03_Services/service-boundaries.md) — i.e. not asking for pentesting, 24/7 monitoring, or active incident response?

**If it's a clear mismatch** (e.g. explicitly wants a pentest, or is an enterprise with 500+ staff): respond warmly, explain briefly why it's not a fit, and offer a referral if one is known. Don't book a call purely to be polite — it wastes both parties' time.

**If it's a possible incident in progress:** do not treat as a sales enquiry. Follow the urgent-incident guidance in [escalation-and-referral-rules.md](escalation-and-referral-rules.md) immediately.

### 3. Book the discovery call
Offer 2–3 concrete time slots within the next 3–5 business days. Use a booking tool (see [../10_Admin/tools-and-software.md](../10_Admin/tools-and-software.md)) to avoid back-and-forth.

### 4. Send pre-call information
At least 24 hours before the call, send:
- Confirmation of time and video link (or phone number)
- A one-line reminder of what to expect ("a focused 15-minute conversation about your business and where cyber security fits in, no pressure, no obligation")
- Optionally, a link to the relevant service page if they mentioned a specific interest

### 5. Log the enquiry
Record in the CRM/lead tracker (see [tools-and-software.md](../10_Admin/tools-and-software.md)): source, date, business name, contact name, initial need, and call date.

## What "good" looks like

- Every enquiry gets a response within 1 business day, every time
- No prospect waits more than 5 business days to get a discovery call booked
- Clear mismatches are turned away kindly and quickly, not left hanging or over-promised

## Assumptions / needs founder input

- Choice of booking tool (Calendly or similar) and CRM/lead tracker (spreadsheet vs. lightweight CRM) — see [tools-and-software.md](../10_Admin/tools-and-software.md).
