# Management Action Register Template

*Internal tracking instrument, distinct from the client-facing [Client Action Plan](../05_Client_Templates/client-action-plan-template.md). See [remediation-and-verification.md](remediation-and-verification.md) for how the two relate. Shared with a client only where they specifically want this level of detail — most clients are better served by the simpler action plan.*

**Status values:** Not started / Planned / In progress / Blocked / Complete (client-reported) / Verified complete (Pharos-confirmed) / Risk accepted.

**Completion evidence:** what the client provided or Pharos observed to support a status change — a screenshot, a policy document, a confirmation from the IT provider. Left blank until something exists; never filled in retrospectively to match a claimed status.

**Pharos verification:** left blank until Pharos has actually checked. "Complete" (client-reported) and "Verified complete" (Pharos-confirmed) are recorded as genuinely different statuses — see `remediation-and-verification.md`.

**Risk accepted:** used when the business has made an informed decision not to act on a finding. Record who made that call and why, briefly — this protects both the business and Pharos from the finding being silently forgotten rather than deliberately deprioritised.
