# Proposal Process (SOP)

Turns a discovery call into a written, priced proposal the client can say yes to.

## Timing

Send within **2 business days** of the discovery call — ideally next day. Speed signals professionalism and keeps momentum; a proposal that arrives a week later has usually lost most of its heat.

## Steps

### 1. Confirm scope internally
Using discovery call notes, decide:
- Which service(s) fit (reference [../03_Services/service-catalogue.md](../03_Services/service-catalogue.md))
- Any scope adjustments needed for this client's specific size/complexity
- Price within the suggested range (see [../02_Business_Strategy/pricing-strategy.md](../02_Business_Strategy/pricing-strategy.md)) — document the reasoning if it's at the high or low end

### 2. Draft the proposal email
Use [../06_Sales_and_Marketing/proposal-email-template.md](../06_Sales_and_Marketing/proposal-email-template.md) as the base. Every proposal must include:
- A one-line recap of what was discussed (shows you listened)
- Recommended service(s), in plain English
- What's included and explicitly **what's not included** (reference [../03_Services/service-boundaries.md](../03_Services/service-boundaries.md) language)
- Price (fixed, not hourly, wherever the service allows it)
- Timeline: how soon it can start, how long delivery takes
- Payment terms (see [../02_Business_Strategy/pricing-strategy.md](../02_Business_Strategy/pricing-strategy.md))
- A clear, low-friction next step ("reply to this email or click here to confirm")

### 3. Attach or link the Terms of Engagement
Every proposal above a minimal threshold (e.g. anything beyond a single short session) should reference or attach the standard Terms of Engagement. *[Lawyer-drafted template pending — see [../02_Business_Strategy/risk-boundaries.md](../02_Business_Strategy/risk-boundaries.md).]*

### 4. Send and log
Send the proposal, log the date and value in the CRM/tracker, and set a follow-up reminder (see [follow-up-email-template.md](../06_Sales_and_Marketing/follow-up-email-template.md)) for **5 business days** later if no response.

### 5. Handle the response
- **Accepted:** send the deposit invoice, confirm the Terms of Engagement is agreed, schedule kickoff, move to [delivery-workflow.md](delivery-workflow.md)
- **Questions/negotiation:** respond promptly and honestly; it's fine to adjust scope to fit budget (see discounting policy in [../02_Business_Strategy/pricing-strategy.md](../02_Business_Strategy/pricing-strategy.md)), but hold the line on the value of the work
- **Declined or silent:** send one follow-up after 5 business days, then one more after a further 10 business days, then stop chasing (see [follow-up-email-template.md](../06_Sales_and_Marketing/follow-up-email-template.md)) — log as "not proceeding" and revisit in 6 months

## What "good" looks like

- Every discovery call gets a proposal within 2 business days
- Every proposal states price and exclusions clearly — no ambiguity that leads to scope disputes later
- Win rate and average time-to-close are tracked so the process can be improved

## Assumptions / needs founder input

- No lawyer-reviewed Terms of Engagement exists yet — treat this as a launch-blocking item (see [../10_Admin/next-actions.md](../10_Admin/next-actions.md)).
- Deposit percentage (suggested 50%) should be confirmed as the founder's actual cash-flow preference.
