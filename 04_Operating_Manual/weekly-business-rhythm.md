# Weekly Business Rhythm

A suggested weekly structure for a solo founder balancing delivery, sales, and operations. Adjust freely — the point is having *some* deliberate rhythm rather than reacting to whatever's loudest each day.

## Suggested weekly time allocation (illustrative)

| Category | % of working week | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Client delivery (billable) | 45–55% | Actual engagement work — reviews, workshops, report writing |
| Sales & business development | 20–25% | Discovery calls, proposals, follow-ups, referral outreach |
| Marketing & content | 10–15% | LinkedIn posts, checklist/lead magnet updates, website tweaks |
| Admin & operations | 10–15% | Invoicing, CRM updates, tooling, planning |

## Suggested weekly rhythm

### Monday — Plan the week
- Review the pipeline (see [../10_Admin/tools-and-software.md](../10_Admin/tools-and-software.md) for CRM/tracker)
- Confirm delivery deadlines for the week
- Block time for any scheduled discovery calls, workshops, or sessions
- Send any overdue proposals or follow-ups from last week

### Tuesday–Thursday — Delivery focus
- Protect the majority of these days for actual client work: discovery sessions, technical reviews, report writing, workshops
- Batch admin tasks (invoicing, scheduling replies) rather than context-switching constantly

### Friday — Business development and marketing
- Discovery calls if scheduling allows
- Write/schedule the week's LinkedIn post(s) (see [../06_Sales_and_Marketing/linkedin-post-ideas.md](../06_Sales_and_Marketing/linkedin-post-ideas.md))
- Referral partner outreach or check-ins
- Review any completed engagements from the week: send feedback requests, request testimonials, log lessons learned

## Monthly rhythm additions

- **First week of the month:** review the previous month's numbers — discovery calls held, proposals sent, win rate, revenue, retainer client count. Compare against [../02_Business_Strategy/90-day-launch-plan.md](../02_Business_Strategy/90-day-launch-plan.md) targets (or later, annual targets).
- **Mid-month:** check retainer client health — is response time being met? Any retainer client under-served or over-capacity?
- **Month-end:** invoicing reconciliation, update the [document-control-register.md](../10_Admin/document-control-register.md) if any templates were revised during the month.

## Quarterly rhythm additions

- Revisit [pricing-strategy.md](../02_Business_Strategy/pricing-strategy.md) against real data (actual time spent vs. estimated effort per service)
- Revisit [customer-profiles.md](../02_Business_Strategy/customer-profiles.md) and [competitor-positioning.md](../02_Business_Strategy/competitor-positioning.md) with real market learnings
- Review capacity: is retainer client load sustainable alongside project work? (See [../03_Services/security-adviser.md](../03_Services/security-adviser.md).)

## Capacity guardrails

- Set a hard cap on concurrent active project engagements (suggested starting point: **3–4 concurrent projects** for a solo founder, adjusted once real effort data exists)
- Set a hard cap on retainer clients relative to available monthly hours (suggested starting point: **6–10 retainer clients** at the Essentials/Standard mix, fewer if weighted toward Priority tier)
- If capacity is consistently maxed, that's a signal to either raise prices, bring on an associate, or both — not to quietly over-deliver past sustainable limits

## Assumptions / needs founder input

- Time allocation percentages and capacity caps are starting assumptions — replace with real numbers after the first full quarter of delivery.
