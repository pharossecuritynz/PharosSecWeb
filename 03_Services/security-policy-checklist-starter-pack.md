# Security Policy and Checklist Starter Pack

## Purpose

Provides straightforward, plain-English security policies and checklists tailored to how a specific business actually works — closing a common gap where SMEs need *something* written down (for insurance, customers, or their own governance) but have no realistic path to producing it themselves.

## Who it's for

Businesses that need basic documented policies — often triggered by a customer security questionnaire, cyber insurance renewal, or a general sense that "we should have something written down" — but don't have the time, expertise, or appetite to write formal policy documents from scratch.

## Problems it solves

- "A customer/insurer asked for our security policy and we don't have one."
- "We know we should have basic policies but writing them ourselves feels overwhelming."
- "We don't want a 40-page policy nobody will ever read."

## Scope

A tailored set of short, practical documents, typically including:
- Acceptable Use Policy (staff-facing, plain English)
- Password and Authentication Policy
- Device and Remote Working Policy
- Data Handling and Classification basics
- Incident Reporting Policy (staff-facing "if you think something's wrong, do this")
- A one-page Security Checklist summarising the above for quick reference

Exact document set is agreed during scoping — not every business needs every document.

## Out of scope

- Full ISO 27001 / SOC 2 documentation sets (a much larger undertaking — refer to a compliance specialist if a client needs formal certification-ready documentation)
- Legal review of policy documents (recommend client has policies reviewed by a lawyer before being treated as binding employment policy, particularly the Acceptable Use Policy)
- Ongoing policy maintenance (can be included under [Ongoing Cyber Advisor Support](ongoing-cyber-advisor-support.md))

## Deliverables

1. Short scoping conversation to confirm which documents are needed and tailor content to the business
2. Drafted policy set (Word/PDF format, client's branding applied where wanted)
3. One-page Security Checklist summary
4. A short call to walk through the documents and how to roll them out to staff

## Inputs required from client

- Confirmation of which documents are needed (guided by Pharos Security's recommendation)
- Basic business details: company name, any existing informal practices to reflect, branding assets if documents are to be branded
- Nominated internal owner for policy rollout and future updates

## Delivery process

1. Scoping/proposal — confirm document set
2. Drafting (allow 3–5 business days depending on number of documents)
3. Draft review with client, incorporate feedback
4. Final documents delivered
5. Rollout walkthrough call

## Estimated effort (founder time)

| Task | Time |
|---|---|
| Scoping/proposal | 0.5 hour |
| Drafting (per document, average) | 1–1.5 hours |
| Review & revision | 1 hour |
| Rollout call | 0.5 hour |
| **Total (typical 4–5 document set)** | **~6–9 hours** |

## Suggested pricing range

**NZD $500 – $1,200** (excl. GST) for a typical starter set (4–5 documents); price scales with number of documents and degree of customisation. See [../02_Business_Strategy/pricing-strategy.md](../02_Business_Strategy/pricing-strategy.md).

## Risks and limitations

- Policies are only useful if actually rolled out and followed — recommend pairing with a [Staff Cyber Awareness Session](staff-cyber-awareness-sessions.md) to introduce them properly
- Templates must be genuinely tailored, not generic boilerplate with the company name swapped in — generic policies undermine the "plain-English, right-sized" brand promise
- Recommend legal review before use as binding HR/employment policy

## Sales copy
