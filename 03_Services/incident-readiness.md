# Incident Readiness

**Broadened and renamed from Incident Readiness Workshop, 2026-08-22, under the Pharos v2 directive.** The workshop remains the core delivery format but is now one deliverable within a family, not the whole service. Previous version archived at `archive/incident-readiness-workshop.md`. See `00-business/decisions.md`.

## Purpose

Helps a business prepare for an incident before one happens: preparation, coordination, documentation, communication, and decision-making, not technical response.

## Who it's for

Businesses with no documented plan for handling a cyber incident, who recognise that figuring it out during a crisis is worse than preparing in advance.

## Problems it solves

- "We have no idea what we'd actually do if we got hit by ransomware or a breach."
- "We don't know who to call, in what order, or what we're expected to do."
- "We want our team to have practiced this, even briefly, before it's real."
- "What would happen if somebody compromised our email tomorrow?"

## Scope and possible deliverables

Scoped to the business; not every client needs every item. The available set:

- Incident Response Plan
- Incident Contacts Sheet
- First 60 Minutes Checklist
- Incident Decision Guide
- Communications Checklist
- Ransomware Response Considerations
- Privacy Breach Checklist
- Tabletop Exercise (the core half-day facilitated session)
- Lessons and Improvement Plan (following a real incident or an exercise)

## Boundaries

Pharos prepares clients before an incident and helps them stay organised during one. Pharos is not currently positioned as a full digital forensics and incident response firm. During a serious incident, specialist providers may be needed for forensic imaging, malware reverse engineering, complex compromise investigation, ransomware negotiation, specialist recovery, or legal and privacy-legal interpretation.

During a live incident, Pharos can help by:

- activating the plan
- coordinating stakeholders
- documenting events
- helping management maintain structure
- liaising with specialist providers
- keeping actions organised

Pharos cannot lead technical remediation or forensic investigation. Live incidents should be reported to the **NCSC** (ncsc.govt.nz/report, 0800 114 115) and, where financial fraud is involved, the client's bank and NZ Police. See `02_Business_Strategy/risk-boundaries.md`.

## Deliverables (typical engagement)

1. Half-day facilitated Tabletop Exercise with key decision-makers (owner, ops lead, IT provider if possible), walking through realistic scenarios (ransomware, business email compromise, lost device, a data breach involving customer data)
2. The Incident Response Plan and Incident Contacts Sheet (see `05_Client_Templates/incident-readiness-plan-template.md`)
3. First 60 Minutes Checklist and "who to call" quick-reference card (see `09_Checklists/incident-readiness-checklist.md`)
4. Where relevant: Privacy Breach Checklist, referencing Privacy Act 2020 obligations without providing legal advice on them

## Inputs required from client

- Availability of key decision-makers for the workshop session
- Existing contact details for IT provider, insurer, bank, and legal counsel where applicable
- Any existing, even informal, notes on what they'd currently do in an incident

## Delivery process

1. Scoping and proposal, confirm attendees, format, and which deliverables are needed
2. Pre-workshop preparation: tailor scenarios to the business's actual risk profile
3. Workshop delivery
4. Plan drafting from workshop outputs (allow 2 to 3 business days)
5. Draft review with client
6. Final plan and reference materials delivered

## Suggested pricing range

**NZD $900 – $2,000** (excl. GST), depending on group size and deliverable set. See `02_Business_Strategy/pricing-strategy.md`.

## Risks and limitations

- A plan is only as good as its last review; recommend an annual revisit or one after any significant business change
- Effectiveness depends on the right decision-makers actually attending
- Must stay explicit that this is preparation and coordination support, not a substitute for professional incident response during a real event

## Sales copy

> Nobody thinks clearly in the middle of a crisis. Incident Readiness gets your key people in a room, or on a call, before anything's gone wrong, walks through what a real incident might look like for your business, and leaves you with a clear, calm plan, so if it ever happens, you already know what to do and who to call.
