# Security Adviser

**Renamed from Ongoing Cyber Advisor Support, 2026-08-22, under the Pharos v2 directive.** Not positioned as vCISO at this stage; the brief is explicit that this should grow naturally toward fractional security leadership over time rather than claim it now. Previous version archived at `archive/ongoing-cyber-advisor-support.md`. See `00-business/decisions.md`.

## Purpose

Provides continued access to independent, practical cyber security advice as a business's tools, team, and risks evolve, the recurring relationship that follows an initial engagement.

## Positioning line

Independent security guidance without employing a security team.

## Who it's for

Businesses that have completed an initial engagement (Independent Security Review, Secure Foundations, or similar) and want an ongoing independent adviser to check in with as things change.

## Problems it solves

- "We don't want to start from scratch every time a question comes up."
- "We want someone independent to sense-check new tools or vendors before we commit."
- "Our security posture will drift without someone checking in periodically."
- "We want a named adviser we can call, not a ticket queue."

## Scope

Tiered monthly retainer (see `02_Business_Strategy/pricing-strategy.md` for tier detail), generally including:

- ongoing email or phone access for ad hoc questions
- periodic check-in calls, quarterly at minimum, monthly for higher tiers
- review of the improvement plan and completed actions, with priorities refreshed
- review of new vendors, tools, and MSP proposals from a risk perspective
- review of customer security questionnaires and cyber insurance renewal questions
- discussion of new or significant vulnerabilities relevant to the business
- light maintenance of a security risk register
- an annual mini risk review to catch drift since the last full review
- leadership briefings and tabletop exercise planning where useful
- priority access for one-off project work at a discounted rate

## Out of scope

- 24/7 availability or emergency response SLAs; this is an advisory retainer, not incident response
- Hands-on technical implementation; advisory only, implementation stays with the client's IT provider
- Unlimited scope creep; larger projects are scoped and quoted separately, at the discounted retainer client rate

## Deliverables

- Ongoing availability per the agreed tier's response time
- Quarterly, or monthly, check-in call with brief written notes
- Annual mini risk review summary
- Light-touch advisory input on ad hoc questions

## Inputs required from client

- A nominated primary contact for the relationship
- Willingness to loop Pharos in on relevant changes so advice stays current

## Delivery process

1. Retainer proposal, confirm tier and start date
2. Onboarding call to set expectations and cadence
3. Ongoing delivery per tier
4. Annual mini risk review
5. Quarterly review of the relationship itself: is the tier still the right fit?

## Estimated effort (founder time)

| Task | Time (monthly average) |
|---|---|
| Ad hoc advice | 1–3 hours |
| Check-in call | 1 hour |
| Admin/coordination | 0.5 hour |
| **Total (typical month)** | **~2.5–4.5 hours** |

Annual mini risk review adds roughly 3 to 4 hours in the month it occurs.

## Suggested pricing range

**NZD $500 – $1,500 / month**, tiered (Essentials / Standard / Priority). Deliberately well below NZ vCISO retainers ($3,000 to $12,000/month); this is lighter-touch advisory access, not board-level security leadership. See `02_Business_Strategy/pricing-strategy.md`.

## Risks and limitations

- Capacity is the main operational risk; retainer clients must be capped at a sustainable number relative to project work capacity
- Must stay clear this is not an emergency or incident response service
- Scope creep risk is real; hold the line on ad hoc advice versus a project that needs its own quote

## Sales copy

> Cyber risk doesn't stand still, new tools, new hires, new questions. Security Adviser means you've always got an independent, trusted voice to call before a decision gets made, not just after something's gone wrong.

## Report/output structure

No single report; value is delivered via check-in call notes and the annual mini risk review summary, a condensed version of the Independent Security Review report structure.

## Assumptions / needs founder input

- Retainer capacity limits should be set explicitly once real time-per-client data exists.
