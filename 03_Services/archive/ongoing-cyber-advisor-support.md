# Ongoing Cyber Advisor Support

## Purpose

Provides continued access to independent, practical cyber security advice as a business's tools, team, and risks evolve — the retainer relationship that follows an initial engagement and builds long-term recurring revenue for the practice.

## Who it's for

Businesses that have completed an initial engagement (Risk Review, Uplift Plan, etc.) and want an ongoing independent advisor to check in with as things change — new systems, new hires, new vendors, or just periodic reassurance that priorities are still right.

## Problems it solves

- "We don't want to start from scratch every time a question comes up."
- "We want someone independent to sense-check new tools/vendors before we commit."
- "Our security posture will drift without someone checking in periodically."
- "We want a named advisor we can call, not a ticket queue."

## Scope

Tiered monthly retainer (see [../02_Business_Strategy/pricing-strategy.md](../02_Business_Strategy/pricing-strategy.md) for tier detail), generally including:
- Ongoing email/phone access for ad hoc questions
- Periodic check-in calls (quarterly at minimum, monthly for higher tiers)
- Light involvement in reviewing new tool/vendor decisions from a risk perspective
- An annual mini risk review to catch drift since the last full review
- Priority access for one-off project work at a discounted rate

## Out of scope

- 24/7 availability or emergency response SLAs (this is an advisory retainer, not incident response — see [../02_Business_Strategy/risk-boundaries.md](../02_Business_Strategy/risk-boundaries.md))
- Hands-on technical implementation (advisory only; implementation stays with the client's IT provider)
- Unlimited scope creep — the retainer covers advisory time, not open-ended project work (larger projects are scoped and quoted separately, at the discounted retainer client rate)

## Deliverables

- Ongoing availability per the agreed tier's response time
- Quarterly (or monthly) check-in call with brief written notes
- Annual mini risk review summary
- Light-touch advisory input on ad hoc questions (email or call)

## Inputs required from client

- A nominated primary contact for the retainer relationship
- Willingness to loop Pharos Security in on relevant changes (new systems, new hires with system access, etc.) so advice stays current

## Delivery process

1. Retainer proposal, confirm tier and start date
2. Onboarding call to set expectations and cadence
3. Ongoing delivery per tier (check-ins, ad hoc advice)
4. Annual mini risk review
5. Quarterly review of the relationship itself — is the tier still the right fit?

## Estimated effort (founder time)

| Task | Time (monthly average) |
|---|---|
| Ad hoc advice (email/calls) | 1–3 hours |
| Check-in call (when scheduled) | 1 hour |
| Admin/coordination | 0.5 hour |
| **Total (typical month)** | **~2.5–4.5 hours** |

Annual mini risk review adds ~3–4 hours in the month it occurs.

## Suggested pricing range

**NZD $500 – $1,500 / month**, tiered (Essentials / Standard / Priority). This sits deliberately well below NZ vCISO retainers ($3,000–$12,000/month) — this is lighter-touch advisory access, not board-level security leadership. See [../02_Business_Strategy/pricing-strategy.md](../02_Business_Strategy/pricing-strategy.md#market-validation) for tier breakdown and market comparison.

## Risks and limitations

- The biggest operational risk is **capacity** — retainer clients must be capped at a sustainable number relative to project work capacity (recommend reviewing total retainer load monthly; see [../04_Operating_Manual/weekly-business-rhythm.md](../04_Operating_Manual/weekly-business-rhythm.md))
- Must be crystal clear this is not an emergency/incident response service — restate the boundary in the retainer agreement itself
- Scope creep risk is real — hold the line on what's "ad hoc advice" vs. "a project that needs its own quote"

## Sales copy

> Cyber risk doesn't stand still — new tools, new hires, new threats. Ongoing Cyber Advisor Support means you've always got an independent, trusted voice to call before a decision gets made, not just after something's gone wrong.

## Report/output structure

No single report — ongoing value is delivered via check-in call notes and the annual mini risk review summary (a condensed version of the [Cyber Risk Review Report](../05_Client_Templates/cyber-risk-review-report-template.md) structure).
