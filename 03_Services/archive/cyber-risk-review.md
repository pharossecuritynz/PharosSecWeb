# Cyber Risk Review

## Purpose

A structured, independent assessment of a business's current cyber risk — giving the owner a clear, honest picture of where they stand and what matters most, without technical jargon.

## Who it's for

SME owners and operations leads who know cyber security matters but have no clear picture of their actual risk, and want an independent, non-technical starting point. Typically the **first engagement** with a new client.

## Problems it solves

- "We don't know if we're doing enough."
- "We don't know what to prioritise."
- "Our IT provider handles security, but we've never had it independently assessed."
- "We need something to show our insurer/board/customer that we've assessed our risk."

## Scope

- Structured discovery covering business context, systems, data, third parties, and existing controls
- Review of key areas: identity & access, device management, email & phishing exposure, backups & recovery, cloud platform configuration (high-level), staff practices, incident readiness, vendor/third-party risk
- Risk prioritisation against likelihood and business impact
- A plain-English report with a prioritised action list

## Out of scope

- Technical penetration testing or vulnerability scanning
- Deep technical configuration review of every system (covered by narrower reviews, e.g. Microsoft 365 Basics Review)
- Implementation of any recommended changes (covered by the SME Security Uplift Plan or the client's IT provider)

## Deliverables

1. Discovery session (interview-based, ~90 minutes)
2. Cyber Risk Review Report (see [../05_Client_Templates/cyber-risk-review-report-template.md](../05_Client_Templates/cyber-risk-review-report-template.md)) including:
   - Executive summary (1 page, non-technical)
   - Risk findings by area, rated by priority
   - Prioritised action list (Now / Next / Later)
3. A 45–60 minute walkthrough call to present findings and answer questions

## Inputs required from client

- Completed [discovery questionnaire](../05_Client_Templates/discovery-questionnaire.md) ahead of the session
- Access to relevant staff (owner, ops lead, IT provider contact if applicable) for the discovery interview
- High-level list of systems/platforms in use (can be informal)

## Delivery process

1. Scoping and proposal (see [../04_Operating_Manual/proposal-process.md](../04_Operating_Manual/proposal-process.md))
2. Client completes discovery questionnaire
3. Discovery session (interview, 60–90 min)
4. Analysis and report writing (allow 3–5 business days)
5. Findings walkthrough call
6. Report delivered in final form (PDF), with a copy of the action list in an easily reusable format

## Estimated effort (founder time)

| Task | Time |
|---|---|
| Scoping/proposal | 1 hour |
| Discovery session | 1.5 hours |
| Analysis & report writing | 4–7 hours |
| Findings walkthrough | 1 hour |
| **Total** | **~7.5–10.5 hours** |

## Suggested pricing range

**NZD $1,800 – $4,200** (excl. GST), scaled by business size/complexity. This is in line with published NZ market data — baseline SME security assessments start around $2,000, and this range stays well clear of penetration testing pricing ($5,000–$20,000+) to reinforce that boundary. See [../02_Business_Strategy/pricing-strategy.md](../02_Business_Strategy/pricing-strategy.md#market-validation).

## Risks and limitations

- Findings are based on what the client reports and what's observable without technical testing — genuinely hidden technical vulnerabilities may not surface (state this clearly in the report)
- Not a substitute for penetration testing if the client's risk profile warrants it (flag as a referral if relevant)
- Quality depends heavily on client engagement in the discovery session — a rushed or incomplete session weakens the output

## Sales copy

> Most businesses know cyber security matters but have never had an honest, independent look at where they actually stand. The Cyber Risk Review gives you a clear picture — in plain English — of what's working, what's not, and what to prioritise next. No jargon, no scare tactics, just clarity.
