# Email and Phishing Readiness

## Purpose

Reduces a business's exposure to the most common way SMEs are compromised — email-based attacks (phishing, business email compromise, invoice fraud) — through a combination of technical review and practical, non-alarming guidance.

## Who it's for

Businesses that handle payments, invoices, or sensitive communication by email (i.e. almost all SMEs) and are concerned about phishing, fake invoice scams, or business email compromise — often triggered by a near-miss or an incident at a peer business.

## Problems it solves

- "We nearly paid a fake invoice — how do we stop that happening again?"
- "We're not sure our email is properly protected against spoofing."
- "Our staff aren't confident spotting suspicious emails."

## Scope

- Technical review of email authentication (SPF, DKIM, DMARC configuration)
- Review of email platform anti-phishing settings (where the platform is Microsoft 365, this overlaps with but is narrower than the full [Microsoft 365 Basics Review](microsoft-365-security-basics-review.md))
- Review of financial process controls around invoice and payment approval (a process, not just technical, control)
- Practical guidance materials: a phishing recognition guide and a response cheat sheet (see [../09_Checklists/phishing-response-checklist.md](../09_Checklists/phishing-response-checklist.md))
- Optional: a light-touch simulated phishing test (a small number of realistic, non-punitive test emails to gauge current staff readiness) — **only offered where the founder is confident delivering this ethically and the client explicitly agrees to the approach in advance**

## Out of scope

- Ongoing automated phishing simulation platforms (this is a one-off readiness engagement, not a recurring simulation service — that can be discussed under [Ongoing Cyber Advisor Support](ongoing-cyber-advisor-support.md) if there's demand)
- Forensic investigation of a phishing incident that has already occurred (see [../02_Business_Strategy/risk-boundaries.md](../02_Business_Strategy/risk-boundaries.md))
- Legal advice on fraud recovery

## Deliverables

1. Technical email authentication review
2. Financial process review (short interview with whoever manages invoicing/payments)
3. Phishing and Email Readiness Report (see [../05_Client_Templates/phishing-readiness-report-template.md](../05_Client_Templates/phishing-readiness-report-template.md))
4. Phishing Response Cheat Sheet (client-ready, can be printed/pinned near desks)
5. Optional: light simulated test summary, if agreed in advance

## Inputs required from client

- Access to email platform admin settings (or IT provider cooperation)
- 30-minute conversation with whoever manages invoicing/payments about current approval process
- Written agreement in advance if a simulated phishing test is included

## Delivery process

1. Scoping/proposal — confirm whether simulated testing is included
2. Technical review of email authentication settings
3. Financial process interview
4. Report and cheat sheet prepared (allow 2–3 business days)
5. Findings call
6. Report and materials delivered

## Risks and limitations

- Technical review reduces but cannot eliminate phishing risk — human behaviour remains the largest variable (pair with [Staff Cyber Awareness Sessions](staff-cyber-awareness-sessions.md) for the biggest impact)
- Simulated phishing testing must be handled sensitively — framed as a learning exercise, never as a "gotcha" or performance-management tool. Get explicit client sign-off on approach and communication to staff beforehand.

Email and Phishing Readiness checks your technical defences, reviews how your team handles payments, and leaves you with practical, plain-English guidance your whole team can actually use.
