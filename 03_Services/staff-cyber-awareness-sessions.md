# Staff Cyber Awareness Sessions

## Purpose

Plain-language, facilitated training that builds safer everyday habits across a team — without jargon, fear tactics, or generic e-learning modules nobody remembers.

## Who it's for

Businesses that recognise their people are both their biggest risk and their biggest defence, and want a genuinely engaging, business-specific alternative to generic online training platforms.

## Problems it solves

- "Our staff have done generic online security training and it hasn't changed anything."
- "We want our team to actually understand *why* this matters, not just tick a compliance box."
- "We're growing fast and onboarding people faster than our habits are keeping up."

## Scope

- A facilitated session (typically 60–90 minutes), in-person or remote, for up to ~20 staff per session (larger teams split into multiple sessions)
- Content tailored to the business's actual context and recent real-world examples (anonymised where drawn from other engagements)
- Core topics: recognising phishing and social engineering, password and MFA habits, safe use of personal devices, safe remote/hybrid work practices, what to do if you think you've made a mistake (blame-free reporting culture)
- Interactive elements (real example walkthroughs, Q&A) rather than a one-way lecture

## Out of scope

- Automated, ongoing e-learning platform delivery (Pharos Security delivers live, facilitated sessions, not a self-serve LMS product)
- Individual staff performance assessment or reporting on named individuals' quiz results back to management (undermines the blame-free culture the training is designed to build)

## Deliverables

1. Tailored session content (built around the specific business)
2. Live facilitated session delivery
3. A one-page staff takeaway summary (see [../09_Checklists/sme-cyber-readiness-checklist.md](../09_Checklists/sme-cyber-readiness-checklist.md) for related content)
4. Brief summary notes to the business owner/ops lead on key themes and any notable gaps observed (without naming individuals)

## Inputs required from client

- Confirmed attendee list and scheduling
- Any specific recent incidents or near-misses the client wants addressed (kept confidential, used only to tailor content)
- Venue/tech setup for in-person sessions, or a suitable video platform for remote delivery

## Delivery process

1. Scoping/proposal, confirm group size, format, and number of sessions needed
2. Content tailoring (allow 1–2 business days)
3. Session delivery
4. Takeaway summary distributed to attendees
5. Brief debrief notes sent to the client

|---|---|
| Scoping/proposal | 0.5 hour |
| Content tailoring | 2–3 hours |
| Session delivery | 1.5 hours |
| Takeaway/debrief prep | 1 hour |
| **Total (per session)** | **~5–6 hours** |

## Suggested pricing range

**NZD $600 – $1,200 per session** (up to ~20 attendees), excl. GST. Multi-session bookings (e.g. for larger teams split across sessions) should carry a modest per-session discount. See [../02_Business_Strategy/pricing-strategy.md](../02_Business_Strategy/pricing-strategy.md).

## Risks and limitations

- One session builds awareness but doesn't guarantee lasting behaviour change — recommend annual refreshers, and mention [Ongoing Cyber Advisor Support](ongoing-cyber-advisor-support.md) as a way to keep reinforcing habits
- Engagement quality depends heavily on facilitation — content must stay conversational and specific, not a generic slide deck
