# Sitemap

## Current (v1): single page

```
/ (homepage — all sections as anchors)
  #services
  #process
  #about
  #contact
```

All navigation is anchor-based scrolling within the single homepage. This is intentional for launch — a focused, fast, easy-to-maintain site while the business is establishing initial credibility and content.

## Future (v2+): multi-page structure

A recommended expansion path once there's enough content and traffic to justify it — not a launch requirement.

```
/                          Homepage (condensed version of current content)
/services                  Services overview (current services section, expanded)
/services/cyber-risk-review            Individual service page
/services/sme-security-uplift-plan     Individual service page
/services/microsoft-365-review         Individual service page
/services/email-phishing-readiness     Individual service page
/services/incident-readiness-workshop  Individual service page
/services/staff-awareness-sessions     Individual service page
/services/policy-starter-pack          Individual service page
/services/ongoing-advisor-support      Individual service page
/about                     Expanded About page (founder story, principles)
/resources                 Lead magnets / checklists hub
/resources/[checklist-slug] Individual resource landing pages
/contact                   Standalone contact page with form
```

## Trigger for moving to multi-page

Consider expanding once:
- Individual services need more explanation than a homepage card allows (e.g. FAQs, detailed process)
- SEO becomes a meaningful acquisition channel (individual service pages target specific search terms better than a single page)
- A resources/content hub is needed to support lead magnet distribution at scale

## URL and SEO notes for future expansion

- Keep URLs lowercase, hyphenated, descriptive (already reflected in the structure above)
- Each service page should target its own specific search intent (e.g. "microsoft 365 security review nz")
- Maintain the homepage as the primary conversion path even after expansion — don't let it become a thin landing page pointing everywhere else

## Assumptions / needs founder input

- Multi-page expansion is a future decision, not a current requirement — revisit after the [90-day-launch-plan.md](../02_Business_Strategy/90-day-launch-plan.md) period based on real traffic and content needs.
