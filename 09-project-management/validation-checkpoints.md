# Validation Checkpoints

Objective, measurable thresholds for deciding whether to double down, continue but adjust, or stop/radically reposition. These are decision inputs, not promises, and not a reason to feel discouraged if the numbers land in the middle. Track the underlying numbers in `10_Admin/commercial-validation-tracker.csv`; update `09-project-management/pharos-assumption-register.md` alongside every checkpoint.

**Start the clock from the first day of genuine, sustained outreach** (not from today, if outreach hasn't actually started yet), and record that start date here once it happens: ______________.

## 30-day checkpoint

### DOUBLE DOWN if

- 20+ meaningful buyer/partner conversations held
- 5+ qualified opportunities identified
- 2+ proposals sent
- At least 1 paid customer (pilot or standard tier)
- Acceptable reaction to $2,000+ pricing in real conversations (not just polite non-objection; genuine engagement with the number)

### CONTINUE BUT ADJUST if

- Meaningful conversations are happening, but volume is below the double-down thresholds
- Some qualified opportunities exist, but positioning or price resistance is unclear or mixed
- Proposals have gone out, but closes are limited or slow

**Action:** revisit positioning, the ICP, and pricing against what real conversations actually revealed, using `09-project-management/pharos-assumption-register.md`. Don't just push harder on the same message; change something specific and testable.

### STOP / RADICALLY REASSESS if

- 15 to 20 genuinely qualified conversations show almost no willingness to pay, or
- Customers overwhelmingly say their MSP already provides sufficient value and mean it, not just as a polite deflection

**Action:** this is a real signal, not a motivation problem. Revisit the ICP, the offer, and the core positioning from first principles before spending more time on outreach using the same approach.

## 90-day checkpoint

### Desired evidence (validation thresholds, not promises)

- 3 to 5+ paid customers
- Cumulative revenue roughly $7,500 to $15,000+
- Proposal win rate trending toward 30%, once there's enough sample size for that number to mean anything (fewer than ~8 to 10 proposals sent, treat the win rate as too noisy to act on alone)
- At least one referral (from a client or a partner)
- At least one follow-on sale (Remediation Assurance, or genuine interest in Security Adviser)
- Realised delivery economics trending toward the $180 to $200+/hour target in `10_Admin/delivery-economics-tracker.csv`, at least on standard-tier (non-pilot) engagements

### If thresholds are broadly met

Move from "prove it works" to "make it repeatable": consider re-opening the folder-taxonomy migration, the remaining service-name rename, and any BUILD LATER service in `03_Services/service-catalogue.md` that real client conversations specifically asked for, in that order of evidence strength.

### If thresholds are partly met

Identify the single weakest point in the funnel (conversations to qualified opportunities, qualified opportunities to proposals, or proposals to paid work) and treat fixing that one stage as the next 30 days' entire focus, rather than spreading effort across everything at once.

### If thresholds are largely missed

Treat this as a real, evidence-based signal to reassess the core offer and ICP, not a reason to build more supporting material or expand the service list. Revisit `00-business/pharos-current-strategy.md` from first principles, using whatever real objections and feedback exist in `10_Admin/commercial-validation-tracker.csv`.

## What these checkpoints deliberately do not measure

Website polish, number of documents written, number of agent specifications built, or founder confidence. None of that is evidence of commercial viability; only real prospect and client behaviour is.
