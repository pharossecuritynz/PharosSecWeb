# Proposal Email Template

*Used per [../04_Operating_Manual/proposal-process.md](../04_Operating_Manual/proposal-process.md). Send within 2 business days of the discovery call.*

---

**Subject:** Your Pharos Security proposal — [Business Name]

Hi [First Name],

Thanks again for the chat [yesterday/on Tuesday] — it was great to learn more about [Business Name] and [something specific they mentioned, e.g. "how you're growing the team this year"].

Based on what we covered, here's what I'd recommend and what it involves.

**What I'd suggest: [Service Name]**

[1–2 sentence plain-English description of the service and why it fits what they described.]

**What's included:**
- [Deliverable 1]
- [Deliverable 2]
- [Deliverable 3]

**What's not included:** [Brief, calm boundary note — e.g. "This review looks at your current setup and practices; it doesn't include penetration testing or ongoing monitoring. Happy to talk through either of those separately if useful down the track."]

**Timeline:** We could start as early as [date], with the final report/plan delivered within [X] of kickoff.

**Investment:** NZD $[X], excl. GST. This covers [brief scope recap]. Payment is 50% on confirmation, 50% on delivery.

**Next step:** If this sounds right, just reply "let's go" and I'll send through the agreement and get us scheduled. Happy to jump on a quick call first if you've got questions — no pressure either way.

Thanks again for your time, [First Name] — looking forward to helping [Business Name] get some clarity here.

[Founder Name]
Pharos Security
Clear guidance. Safer business.
[email] · [phone, if applicable]

---

## Usage notes

- Personalise the opening line every time — never send this as a generic template
- Match price to the actual scope discussed, referencing [../02_Business_Strategy/pricing-strategy.md](../02_Business_Strategy/pricing-strategy.md)
- Attach or link the Terms of Engagement where required (see [../04_Operating_Manual/proposal-process.md](../04_Operating_Manual/proposal-process.md))
- Keep the whole email short enough to read on a phone in under a minute
