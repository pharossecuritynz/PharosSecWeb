# Lead Magnet Ideas

Free, useful resources designed to attract prospects, build trust, and capture contact details before a sales conversation. Full checklist content lives in [../09_Checklists/](../09_Checklists/); this document covers strategy and rollout.

## Prioritised list

| Lead magnet | Format | Priority | Ties to service |
|---|---|---|---|
| SME Cyber Readiness Checklist | 1-page PDF | **1st — build first** | Cyber Risk Review |
| Microsoft 365 Security Basics Checklist | 1-page PDF | 2nd | Microsoft 365 Basics Review |
| Incident Readiness Checklist | 1-page PDF | 3rd | Incident Readiness Workshop |
| Phishing Response Cheat Sheet | 1-page PDF | 4th | Email and Phishing Readiness |
| Cyber Risk Self-Assessment for NZ SMEs | Interactive/scored short form (later: could be a simple web form) | 5th — more effort, higher value | Cyber Risk Review |

## Why the SME Cyber Readiness Checklist comes first

It's the broadest-appeal, lowest-effort-to-produce resource, and maps directly to the primary front-door service. It also gives new LinkedIn content something concrete to point to immediately.

## Distribution strategy

- Website: a simple "Download the checklist" offer, either gated (email required) or ungated depending on the founder's preference for lead capture vs. maximum reach
- LinkedIn: share directly, and reference in relevant posts (see [linkedin-post-ideas.md](linkedin-post-ideas.md))
- Referral partners: give partners a ready-to-forward resource that makes referring easy
- Email signature: link to the checklist as a low-friction value-add

## Gated vs. ungated recommendation

**Recommendation: start ungated** (freely downloadable, no email required) for the first 2–3 lead magnets. At this early stage, building trust and reach matters more than list-building, and a gate adds friction that reduces sharing. Revisit gating once there's enough traffic to make email capture meaningfully useful, and once a simple mailing list tool is in place (see [../10_Admin/tools-and-software.md](../10_Admin/tools-and-software.md)).

## The Cyber Risk Self-Assessment (future, higher-effort option)

A short (10–15 question) self-scoring assessment that gives a business a rough sense of their risk posture and a personalised recommendation of which Pharos Security service fits best. Higher production effort (ideally a simple interactive web form rather than a static PDF) — treat as a Phase 2 project once the core checklists are live and the website has an established audience.

## Design and brand notes

- All lead magnets follow [../01_Brand/visual-identity.md](../01_Brand/visual-identity.md) and [../01_Brand/typography.md](../01_Brand/typography.md)
- Keep every lead magnet genuinely useful standalone — never a teaser designed to feel incomplete without buying something. The goal is trust, not a bait-and-switch.
