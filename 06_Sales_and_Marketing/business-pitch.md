# Pharos Security

**Clear guidance. Safer business.**

*Independent cyber security advisory for New Zealand small and medium businesses.*

---

## The problem

Most New Zealand small and medium businesses already pay someone to keep their technology running: an IT provider, a managed service provider, or an internal IT person with a broad remit. What almost none of them have is an independent way to answer a simple question: is our security actually good enough, and would we know if it wasn't?

That question tends to surface at specific moments: a customer sends a security questionnaire nobody can confidently complete, a cyber insurance renewal asks about controls the business has never reviewed, a near miss with a suspicious invoice or email shakes everyone's confidence, or a board member simply asks "are we doing enough?" Until one of those moments arrives, security has usually been whatever the IT provider happened to bundle in, not something anyone independently checked.

## What Pharos Security does

Pharos Security helps small New Zealand businesses understand whether sensible security controls are actually in place, and what to do next.

**Your IT provider manages your technology. Pharos helps you know whether your security is good enough.**

That is the whole relationship. Pharos does not sell software, does not provide managed IT services, and does not replace the IT provider a business already has. What it sells is:

- an independent, outside view of where the business actually stands
- clear prioritisation, so limited time and budget go to what matters most
- practical, achievable recommendations, not an enterprise checklist
- a second opinion on what an IT provider or vendor is proposing
- an honest account of what was checked, and how confident that check makes us
- ongoing access to independent advice, for businesses that want it

Reviews and reports are how that gets delivered. They are not the product itself.

## How Pharos does it

Every engagement follows the same disciplined, plain-English process:

1. **Understand the business.** What it does, what it depends on, and who its customers and regulators are, before looking at a single setting.
2. **Gather evidence.** Every claim is checked against a practical security baseline built from recognised international controls and New Zealand guidance, scaled to what a small business can realistically maintain.
3. **Be precise about certainty.** A finding based on something a client mentioned is reported differently to one Pharos has directly seen or independently tested. Reports say which is which, rather than implying more certainty than the work actually supports.
4. **Translate into business terms.** Every material finding is explained the same way: what was found, what it actually means for the business, and what to do about it. No unexplained jargon, no severity scores without context.
5. **Prioritise, don't overwhelm.** Findings are sorted into Now, Next, and Later, weighed against real cost, effort, and business impact, not presented as a flat list of forty equally urgent items.
6. **Support the plan.** Where useful, Pharos helps the business and its existing IT provider work through the plan together.
7. **Revisit.** Progress is checked against what was actually done, not just what was intended.

Throughout, the tone stays calm and specific. No breach statistics used as pressure, no manufactured urgency, and a plain acknowledgement of what a business has already done well before pointing out what's missing.

## Who needs this

Pharos works with New Zealand organisations roughly **5 to 50 staff** that:

- run on Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace and are generally cloud-heavy
- have limited or no dedicated security staff
- rely on an outsourced IT provider or a generalist internal IT person, not a security specialist
- are led by an owner or manager who takes security seriously but isn't equipped to judge it
- face growing customer, insurance, or contractual security expectations

**Typical buyers:** owners, managing directors, general managers, COOs, CFOs, operations managers, practice managers, and board members.

**Common moments that prompt a conversation:** a customer security questionnaire the business can't confidently answer; a cyber insurance renewal with unfamiliar terms; a suspicious email or a near miss; getting a new IT provider, or leaving one; being quoted for security software without knowing whether it's actually needed; or simply wanting reassurance before something goes wrong.

**Best natural fit:** professional services (law, accounting, financial advisory), property and construction, healthcare and allied health, retail and e-commerce, trades and services businesses with growing back-office systems, and not-for-profits handling sensitive member or donor data.

## How it benefits them

- **A plain-English picture of where they stand.** No report that needs an IT person to translate it back into English.
- **Confidence that advice is unbiased.** Pharos has no product to sell and earns no commission from any recommendation, so the same advice stands whether or not it leads to a purchase.
- **Effort spent on what actually matters.** A sequenced Now/Next/Later plan a real business can act on, not an exhaustive framework nobody has time to complete.
- **A credible answer when someone asks.** Findings are evidence-based and clearly labelled by how they were checked, useful when a customer, insurer, or board member asks what's actually been done.
- **A better working relationship with their existing IT provider.** Pharos clarifies what's already being handled well and what isn't, so conversations with the IT provider are specific and constructive rather than guesswork.
- **Somewhere to turn as things change.** Ongoing access to independent advice as the business grows, adopts new tools, or faces new requirements.

## How an engagement progresses

1. **Free Security Health Check.** A short self-serve questionnaire, at no cost, giving a first indicative read on where things stand.
2. **A short conversation.** A structured discussion to understand the business and confirm the right starting point.
3. **An entry-point review**, matched to what the business needs: an external-only Exposure Snapshot, a full Independent Security Review, or an IT Provider Security Assurance review that independently checks the security outcome of an existing IT arrangement.
4. **Acting on findings**, through a coordinated Secure Foundations improvement period and, where relevant, Incident Readiness planning for "what do we do if..."
5. **An ongoing relationship**, through Security Adviser, for businesses that want continued access to independent advice as things change.

Pricing is discussed directly and depends on scope; it is intentionally left out of this document.

## Why Pharos, and not the alternatives

| Alternative | How Pharos differs |
|---|---|
| **IT provider / MSP** | Manages and supports the technology day to day. Pharos independently reviews the security outcome of that work. Not a replacement, and not an audit designed to catch the IT provider out. |
| **Enterprise security consultancies** | Built for organisations with dedicated budgets and security teams; their frameworks and pricing don't translate to a 15-person business. Pharos exists specifically for the businesses they don't serve well. |
| **Penetration testing firms** | Answer a narrow technical question that's often premature before basic controls are confirmed in place. Pharos looks at the wider risk picture first, and refers to a specialist tester when that's genuinely the right next step. |
| **Security product vendors** | Incentivised to sell their product. Pharos has nothing to sell, so the recommendation doesn't change based on what it results in. |
| **Doing nothing** | The real default. Most SMEs currently rely on whatever their IT provider happens to bundle in, because independent security advice has felt inaccessible or irrelevant to a business their size. |

## For referral partners

If you work with New Zealand SMEs, including as an IT provider, insurance broker, accountant, or lawyer, Pharos is a straightforward, independent option to point clients toward when security questions come up: "are we doing enough?", "what should we prioritise?", "I need something written down for our insurer."

Pharos is not a competitor to what you offer. It doesn't provide IT support, legal advice, or insurance, and it works alongside the relationships a client already has rather than replacing any part of them. There's no formal commission arrangement required to start; the aim is to earn trust through good work first, and to credit and refer back to partners wherever it makes sense.

## What Pharos does not do

An honest limit is part of the offer, not a weakness in it. Pharos does not provide:

- 24-hour monitoring or a security operations centre
- penetration testing or active exploitation of live systems
- forensic incident response or evidence handling for legal proceedings
- compliance certification (Pharos can help prepare for one; it doesn't issue it)
- legal advice on breach notification, contracts, or liability

Where a business genuinely needs one of these, Pharos says so plainly and refers to a suitable specialist.
