# Service Page Copy

Website-ready copy for each service — short card copy (as used on the homepage) plus longer-form copy suitable for individual service pages if the site expands beyond one page in future.

---

## Cyber Risk Review

**Card copy (homepage):** A clear, independent assessment of where your business stands today and the risks that matter most for your operation.

**Longer copy:** Most businesses know cyber security matters but have never had an honest, independent look at where they actually stand. The Cyber Risk Review gives you a clear picture — in plain English — of what's working, what's not, and what to prioritise next. No jargon, no scare tactics, just clarity you can act on.

## SME Security Uplift Plan

**Card copy:** A practical, prioritised plan to strengthen your defences without unnecessary cost or complexity.

**Longer copy:** Knowing your risks is only half the job — the other half is knowing what to actually do about them, in what order, with the budget and team you've got. The SME Security Uplift Plan turns a list of findings into a realistic roadmap you and your IT provider can actually follow.

## Microsoft 365 Security Basics Review

**Card copy:** A focused check of your Microsoft 365 environment against sensible, well-understood security fundamentals.

**Longer copy:** Most New Zealand SMEs run their business on Microsoft 365 — and most have never had it properly checked. This review is a fast, focused way to find out if the fundamentals are actually switched on, with a clear, prioritised list of quick wins.

## Email and Phishing Readiness

**Card copy:** Practical steps to reduce your exposure to the most common way New Zealand businesses are compromised.

**Longer copy:** Email is still how most small businesses get caught out — a fake invoice, a spoofed email from "the boss," a link that looks legitimate. This review checks your technical defences, looks at how your team handles payments, and leaves you with practical guidance your whole team can actually use.

## Incident Readiness Workshop

**Card copy:** A guided session to prepare your team with a clear, calm plan for how to respond if something goes wrong.

**Longer copy:** Nobody thinks clearly in the middle of a crisis. This workshop gets your key people together before anything's gone wrong, walks through what a real incident might look like for your business, and leaves you with a clear, calm plan — so if it ever happens, you already know what to do.

## Staff Cyber Awareness Sessions

**Card copy:** Plain-language training that builds safer everyday habits across your team, without jargon or scare tactics.

**Longer copy:** Most staff cyber training is forgettable — a compliance checkbox nobody remembers a week later. Our sessions are different: live, plain-English, and built around your actual business, so your team leaves understanding not just what to do, but why it matters.

## Security Policy and Checklist Starter Pack

**Card copy:** Straightforward policies and checklists tailored to how your business actually works day to day.

**Longer copy:** Sometimes you just need something written down — for a customer, an insurer, or your own peace of mind. This starter pack gives you clear, plain-English policies built for how your business actually works, not generic templates nobody will read.

## Ongoing Cyber Advisor Support

**Card copy:** Continued access to independent, practical advice as your business, tools, and risks evolve over time.

**Longer copy:** Cyber risk doesn't stand still — new tools, new hires, new threats. Ongoing Cyber Advisor Support means you've always got an independent, trusted voice to call before a decision gets made, not just after something's gone wrong.
