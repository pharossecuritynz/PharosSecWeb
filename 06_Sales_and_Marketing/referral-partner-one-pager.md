## [Pharos Security wordmark]

### Independent cyber security advisory for New Zealand SMEs

Pharos Security helps New Zealand small and medium businesses understand cyber risk, strengthen practical controls, prepare for incidents, and build safer everyday habits — without jargon or scare tactics.

### Why we might be a good referral fit for your clients

Your clients trust you. When cyber security questions come up — "are we doing enough?", "what should we prioritise?", "we need something written down for our insurer" — we're a straightforward, independent option to point them toward.

**We're not a competitor to what you offer.** We don't provide IT support, legal advice, or insurance — we provide independent risk assessment, planning, and advisory, and we actively work alongside your existing relationship with the client rather than replacing any part of it.

### What we do

- Cyber Risk Reviews — a clear, independent picture of where a business stands
- Microsoft 365 Security Basics Reviews
- Email and Phishing Readiness
- Incident Readiness Workshops
- Staff Cyber Awareness Sessions
- Security Policy and Checklist Starter Packs
- Ongoing Cyber Advisor Support

### What we don't do

We're not a managed security provider, penetration testing firm, or incident response firm — and we'll always say so plainly and refer appropriately when a client needs one of those.

### How referrals work

- No formal commission arrangement required to start — we believe in earning trust through good work first
- We'll always credit and loop in the referring partner appropriately, and look for ways to refer back where it makes sense (e.g. referring clients to your IT support, insurance, or legal services when relevant)
- Happy to discuss a formal referral arrangement once there's a track record together

### Let's talk

If you work with New Zealand SMEs and think there's a fit, I'd welcome a short conversation, no pressure, just an introduction.
