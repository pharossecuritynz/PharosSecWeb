## Problem / positioning section

**Eyebrow:** Why Pharos
**Headline:** Cyber security shouldn't feel overwhelming.

**Body:** Most cyber security advice is written for organisations with dedicated security teams and enterprise budgets. Pharos Security translates it into practical, right-sized steps for how New Zealand SMEs actually operate.

**Three points:**
1. **Too busy running the business** — Security often gets pushed aside for the day-to-day demands of running a growing operation.
2. **Advice that misses the mark** — Guidance is either written for large enterprises with dedicated teams, or too vague to actually act on.
3. **Unsure where to start** — Without a clear picture of what matters most, it's hard to know which risks are worth addressing first.

## Process section

**Eyebrow:** Our approach
**Headline:** A steady, four-step path forward.
**Intro:** Every engagement follows the same considered process — calm, methodical, and grounded in what matters for your business.

1. **Understand** — We build a clear picture of your business, your data, and the risks that actually apply to you.
2. **Prioritise** — We rank what matters most, so effort and budget go toward the risks with the greatest impact.
3. **Improve** — We help you put practical, sustainable controls in place — matched to your team and tools.
4. **Prepare** — We make sure your business knows what to do if something goes wrong, before it happens.

## Built for SMEs section

**Headline:** Built for how New Zealand SMEs actually operate.
**Intro:** Pharos Security exists because most cyber security advice isn't written with businesses like yours in mind. We close that gap.

1. **Right-sized for your business** — No enterprise frameworks bolted onto a ten-person team. Recommendations that fit your actual scale.
2. **Plain-language advice** — Every conversation is grounded in what it means for your business, not technical terminology.
3. **Practical, sensible budgets** — Guidance that respects what SMEs can realistically invest in, prioritised by impact.
4. **Independent, no products to sell** — Advice isn't tied to any software or vendor — only to what genuinely reduces your risk.
