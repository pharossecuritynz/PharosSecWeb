# LinkedIn Post Ideas

A working bank of post concepts, organised by theme. Aim for 1–2 posts per week (see [../04_Operating_Manual/weekly-business-rhythm.md](../04_Operating_Manual/weekly-business-rhythm.md)). All posts should follow [../01_Brand/tone-of-voice.md](../01_Brand/tone-of-voice.md) — genuinely useful, plain-English, no engagement-bait, no fear tactics.

## Theme 1: Plain-English explainers

- "What is MFA, actually — and why does it matter more than a strong password?"
- "SPF, DKIM, DMARC: three acronyms your IT provider might have mentioned. Here's what they actually do, in plain English."
- "What 'least privilege access' means for a 15-person business (not just an enterprise one)."
- "Why 'we have antivirus' isn't the same as 'we're covered.'"

## Theme 2: Practical, right-sized advice

- "Five things worth doing this month if you run a small business and haven't thought about cyber security yet."
- "You don't need a security team to do these three things."
- "The single highest-impact thing most SMEs can do this week (hint: it's not buying software)."
- "What I tell every client about backups — and the one question that reveals if yours would actually work."

## Theme 3: Real (anonymised) scenarios

*Always fully anonymised and generalised — never identifiable, per [../04_Operating_Manual/data-handling-and-confidentiality.md](../04_Operating_Manual/data-handling-and-confidentiality.md).*

- "A client recently asked me: 'Are we a target? We're just a small business.' Here's what I told them."
- "The most common way I see small businesses nearly get caught out — and it's rarely what people expect."
- "A near-miss story (details changed) about a fake invoice — and the one habit that stopped it."

## Theme 4: Myth-busting

- "No, you don't need a Security Operations Centre. Here's what most SMEs actually need first."
- "'We're too small to be a target' — why this is backwards, explained without scare tactics."
- "Cyber insurance isn't a substitute for basic controls — here's why insurers ask the questions they do."

## Theme 5: Behind the business / founder voice

- "Why I started Pharos Security" (launch post — tell the real story once available)
- "Why I don't sell security products — and why that matters for the advice you get."
- "What a typical Cyber Risk Review actually looks like, step by step."

## Theme 6: NZ-specific context

- "What CERT NZ's latest report tells small businesses (not just big ones)."
- "Cyber security and the Privacy Act 2020 — what SMEs actually need to know."
- "Why New Zealand SMEs are an increasingly common target — explained calmly, not with scare stats."

## Format guidance

- Mix short, punchy posts (3–5 sentences) with occasional longer explainer posts
- Use line breaks generously for readability
- End most posts with a genuine, low-pressure invitation to engage ("Curious what others are seeing — what's the most common gap you come across?") rather than a hard CTA every time
- Avoid engagement-bait framing ("You won't believe what happened next...")

## Assumptions / needs founder input

- Real anonymised examples will only exist once client work has started — until then, use plausible, clearly-general scenarios (not framed as real client stories) to avoid misleading readers.
