# NZ Business Setup Checklist

*A practical checklist for legally and operationally establishing Pharos Security in New Zealand. Not legal or accounting advice — consult a professional for final decisions, particularly on structure and tax.*

## Business structure

- [ ] Decide structure: **sole trader** (simplest, but no separation of personal/business liability) vs. **limited company** (via the Companies Office — provides liability separation, small ongoing compliance cost). For an advisory business carrying professional liability risk, a limited company is generally worth the modest extra admin — confirm with an accountant.
- [ ] If a company: register with the [Companies Office](https://www.companiesoffice.govt.nz) (companiesRegister)
- [ ] Choose and confirm the registered business name is available (check both Companies Office and domain availability together)

## Tax and IRD

- [ ] Register for an IRD number for the business (companies need a separate IRD number from the founder's personal one)
- [ ] Decide GST registration timing — mandatory once turnover exceeds $60,000/year, optional (and often advantageous for a B2B services business that can claim GST on expenses) before that threshold. Confirm with an accountant.
- [ ] Set up provisional tax expectations with an accountant once revenue is forecastable
- [ ] Decide on accounting software (see [tools-and-software.md](tools-and-software.md))

## Insurance

- [ ] **Professional Indemnity Insurance** — covers claims arising from advice given (essential for an advisory business)
- [ ] **Public Liability Insurance** — covers third-party injury/property damage (relevant for in-person workshops/sessions)
- [ ] Consider **Cyber Liability Insurance** for the business's own systems, given the nature of the work (some irony in not having this — but a genuine consideration)

## Contracts and legal

- [ ] Engage a commercial lawyer to draft/review: **Terms of Engagement / Statement of Work template**, **Privacy Policy** (for the website), **Website Terms of Use**
- [ ] Confirm intellectual property terms in client contracts (who owns report content, templates, etc. — typically the client owns their specific report, Pharos Security retains rights to its methodology/templates)

## Banking and finance

- [ ] Open a dedicated business bank account (keep separate from personal finances even as a sole trader, for clean bookkeeping)
- [ ] Set up invoicing (see [tools-and-software.md](tools-and-software.md))
- [ ] Set aside a tax savings habit (a fixed % of every payment received, into a separate account) from day one

## Branding and domain

- [ ] Register the domain (pharossecurity.co.nz or confirmed alternative)
- [ ] Set up business email on the registered domain
- [ ] Check trademark availability for "Pharos Security" if brand protection is a priority (IPONZ — Intellectual Property Office of New Zealand)

## Ongoing compliance

- [ ] If a company: annual return to the Companies Office
- [ ] Privacy Act 2020 obligations understood and reflected in operating practice (see [../04_Operating_Manual/data-handling-and-confidentiality.md](../04_Operating_Manual/data-handling-and-confidentiality.md))
- [ ] Consider whether industry-specific registrations apply (none currently identified as mandatory for independent cyber advisory in NZ, but confirm as the business scope solidifies)
