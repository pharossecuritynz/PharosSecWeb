# Tools and Software

A recommended, lightweight toolset for running Pharos Security as a solo founder — chosen for low overhead and to avoid over-tooling a young business. Options are suggestions, not final decisions.

## Core business operations

| Function | Recommended option(s) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Accounting/invoicing | Xero or MYOB | Xero is the NZ SME standard and integrates well with most bookkeeping workflows |
| CRM / lead tracker | A structured spreadsheet initially; Notion or a lightweight CRM (e.g. Pipedrive, HubSpot Free) once volume grows | Don't over-invest in CRM tooling before there's enough pipeline to justify it |
| Scheduling/booking | Calendly or similar | Removes back-and-forth for discovery calls |
| Email | Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 on the business domain | Given the business's own subject matter, MFA must be enabled from day one |
| Password management | 1Password or Bitwarden (business tier) | Especially important given temporary client system access is handled routinely |
| E-signature | PandaDoc, DocuSign, or a simpler tool bundled with the accounting software | For Terms of Engagement / proposal sign-off |

## Delivery and document production

| Function | Recommended option(s) | Notes |
|---|---|---|

## Website and marketing

| Function | Recommended option(s) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Website hosting | Vercel | Natural fit for the Next.js site already built |
| Domain/DNS | Any NZ-accredited registrar (e.g. via a provider supporting .nz domains) | |
| Analytics | Plausible or a similar privacy-respecting analytics tool (rather than a heavier tool), or Google Analytics if simplicity/familiarity wins out | Consider privacy-friendliness given the brand's trust positioning |
| Email marketing (if/when needed) | Mailchimp or a similar lightweight tool | Only needed once lead magnets are gated — see [../06_Sales_and_Marketing/lead-magnet-ideas.md](../06_Sales_and_Marketing/lead-magnet-ideas.md) |
| Social scheduling | Buffer or native LinkedIn scheduling | For consistent posting per [../04_Operating_Manual/weekly-business-rhythm.md](../04_Operating_Manual/weekly-business-rhythm.md) |

| Email authentication checking | Free online tools (e.g. MXToolbox) for SPF/DKIM/DMARC verification | |

## AI tooling

| Function | Recommended option(s) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Drafting assistance | Claude, ChatGPT, or similar, using the prompts in [../07_Agents/](../07_Agents/) | Always human-reviewed before client use, per [../07_Agents/agent-operating-rules.md](../07_Agents/agent-operating-rules.md) |

## Selection principle

Favour tools that are: (1) genuinely necessary at current scale, (2) reputable on security/privacy grounds (given the nature of the business), and (3) cheap or free until volume justifies paid upgrades. Avoid tool sprawl — a solo founder should be able to hold the entire toolset in their head.
