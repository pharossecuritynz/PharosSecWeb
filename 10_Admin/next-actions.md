# Next Actions

The sequenced, practical "start here" action list — pulling together the highest-priority items from across the whole project into one place. See [../02_Business_Strategy/90-day-launch-plan.md](../02_Business_Strategy/90-day-launch-plan.md) for the fuller launch timeline this supports.

## Do first (blocking — before taking any paying client)

1. **Decide business structure** (sole trader vs. limited company) and register it — see [business-setup-checklist-nz.md](business-setup-checklist-nz.md)
2. **Get Professional Indemnity + Public Liability insurance quotes** and bind cover
3. **Engage a commercial lawyer** to draft: Terms of Engagement / Statement of Work template, Privacy Policy, Website Terms of Use
4. **Register the domain** (pharossecurity.co.nz or confirmed alternative) and set up business email
5. **Provide founder bio, background, and headshot** for the About section — see [../06_Sales_and_Marketing/about-page-copy.md](../06_Sales_and_Marketing/about-page-copy.md)

## Do next (within the first 2–3 weeks)

6. **Deploy the website** — connect the domain, choose hosting (Vercel recommended), run through [../09_Checklists/website-launch-checklist.md](../09_Checklists/website-launch-checklist.md)
7. **Set up core tools**: business bank account, accounting/invoicing software, password manager, CRM/tracker, scheduling tool — see [tools-and-software.md](tools-and-software.md)
8. **Validate pricing** — research 5–10 real NZ competitors, sanity-check ranges in [../02_Business_Strategy/pricing-strategy.md](../02_Business_Strategy/pricing-strategy.md)
9. **Finalise the two front-door services** (Cyber Risk Review, Microsoft 365 Basics Review) as fully sellable offers
10. **Set up LinkedIn** (personal profile positioning, optionally a company page) — see [../06_Sales_and_Marketing/linkedin-profile-copy.md](../06_Sales_and_Marketing/linkedin-profile-copy.md)

## Do soon (weeks 3–8)

11. **Publish the SME Cyber Readiness Checklist** as the first lead magnet — see [../09_Checklists/sme-cyber-readiness-checklist.md](../09_Checklists/sme-cyber-readiness-checklist.md)
12. **Reach out to warm contacts** (15–20 people) to introduce the business
13. **Identify and contact 5–10 potential referral partners** — see [../06_Sales_and_Marketing/referral-partner-one-pager.md](../06_Sales_and_Marketing/referral-partner-one-pager.md)
14. **Publish first 4–6 LinkedIn posts** — see [../06_Sales_and_Marketing/linkedin-post-ideas.md](../06_Sales_and_Marketing/linkedin-post-ideas.md)
15. **Run the first discovery calls** and send the first proposals

## Do as the business grows (ongoing)

16. Deliver the first engagements, collect feedback and testimonials
17. Revisit pricing, customer profiles, and competitor positioning with real data after the first 90 days
18. Commission a final professional logo — see [../01_Brand/logo-direction.md](../01_Brand/logo-direction.md)
19. Consider multi-page website expansion once traffic/content justify it — see [../08_Website/sitemap.md](../08_Website/sitemap.md)
20. Set and monitor capacity guardrails as retainer clients accumulate — see [../04_Operating_Manual/weekly-business-rhythm.md](../04_Operating_Manual/weekly-business-rhythm.md)

## A note on sequencing

Items 1–5 are genuinely blocking — don't take a paying client before they're resolved, particularly the Terms of Engagement and insurance. Everything else can move in parallel or be refined iteratively; this project is designed to be a living operating system, not a one-time document set to file away.
