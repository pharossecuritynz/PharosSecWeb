The sequenced, practical "start here" action list — pulling together the highest-priority items from across the whole project into one place. See [../02_Business_Strategy/90-day-launch-plan.md](../02_Business_Strategy/90-day-launch-plan.md) for the fuller launch timeline this supports.

**Status note (2026-09-09):** the current governing priority is commercial validation, not further build; see [../00-business/pharos-current-strategy.md](../00-business/pharos-current-strategy.md) and [../09-project-management/build-now-later-stop.md](../09-project-management/build-now-later-stop.md). The legal/insurance items below (1 to 4) are unchanged and still block taking payment from a real client; they do not block the real prospect and partner conversations the validation plan calls for starting now.

## Do first (blocking — before taking any paying client)

1. **Decide business structure** (sole trader vs. limited company) and register it — see [business-setup-checklist-nz.md](business-setup-checklist-nz.md)
2. **Get Professional Indemnity + Public Liability insurance quotes** and bind cover
3. **Engage a commercial lawyer** to draft: Terms of Engagement / Statement of Work template, Privacy Policy, Website Terms of Use
4. **Register the domain** (pharossecurity.co.nz or confirmed alternative) and set up business email
5. **Provide founder bio, background, and headshot** for the About section — text bio and corrected 26/15-year background figures now live on the website (2026-09-09); headshot still needed. See [../06_Sales_and_Marketing/about-page-copy.md](../06_Sales_and_Marketing/about-page-copy.md)
6. **Brand name due diligence (added 2026-09-09).** Properly check "Pharos Security" against: NZ Companies Office name availability, NZBN, IPONZ trademark registers, domain availability, a Google search for existing competition (including international firms already using "Pharos" in a security-related name), and LinkedIn/company-name competition. Record findings in a new file once done; this is a factual/legal check, not something to resolve by assumption, and do not rename the business pre-emptively based on this note alone.
