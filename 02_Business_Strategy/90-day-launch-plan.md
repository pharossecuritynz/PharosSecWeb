# 90-Day Launch Plan

A realistic, achievable launch sequence for a solo founder. Assumes part-time-to-full-time availability building toward full-time as client work materialises. Adjust timeline if the founder is launching alongside existing employment.

## Days 1–30: Foundation

**Goal: be legally, operationally, and commercially ready to take a paying client.**

- [ ] Confirm business structure and register (see [../10_Admin/business-setup-checklist-nz.md](../10_Admin/business-setup-checklist-nz.md))
- [ ] Register domain, set up business email
- [ ] Get Professional Indemnity + Public Liability insurance quotes and bind cover
- [ ] Engage a lawyer to draft/review Terms of Engagement and a proposal/contract template
- [ ] Finalise pricing (validate [pricing-strategy.md](pricing-strategy.md) ranges)
- [ ] Publish v1 of the website (already built — see [../08_Website/website-brief.md](../08_Website/website-brief.md))
- [ ] Set up core tools (see [../10_Admin/tools-and-software.md](../10_Admin/tools-and-software.md))
- [ ] Finalise the Cyber Risk Review and Microsoft 365 Basics Review as sellable, fully-scoped offers (these are the two front-door services)
- [ ] Set up LinkedIn company page and personal profile positioning (see [../06_Sales_and_Marketing/linkedin-profile-copy.md](../06_Sales_and_Marketing/linkedin-profile-copy.md))

## Days 31–60: First outreach and pipeline building

**Goal: generate the first 5–10 real discovery conversations.**

- [ ] Identify and personally reach out to 15–20 warm contacts (former colleagues, network, local business groups) to introduce the business — not to sell, to inform
- [ ] Reach out to 5–10 potential referral partners (MSPs, insurance brokers, accountants) — see [referral-partner-one-pager.md](../06_Sales_and_Marketing/referral-partner-one-pager.md)
- [ ] Publish first 4–6 LinkedIn posts establishing plain-English expertise (see [linkedin-post-ideas.md](../06_Sales_and_Marketing/linkedin-post-ideas.md))
- [ ] Release the first lead magnet (SME Cyber Readiness Checklist) to start capturing interest
- [ ] Run 5–10 free discovery calls using the [discovery-call-process.md](../04_Operating_Manual/discovery-call-process.md)
- [ ] Send first 3–5 proposals

## Days 61–90: First deliveries and iteration

**Goal: deliver the first paid engagements and refine based on real feedback.**

- [ ] Deliver the first 2–4 paid engagements (aim for at least one Cyber Risk Review completed end-to-end)
- [ ] Collect a testimonial/case study (with client permission) from the first completed engagement
- [ ] Review actual delivery time vs. estimated effort for each service; adjust pricing/scope if needed
- [ ] Revisit [customer-profiles.md](customer-profiles.md) and [competitor-positioning.md](competitor-positioning.md) with real data from conversations had so far
- [ ] Assess pipeline health: how many discovery calls converted to proposals, and proposals to paid work? Identify the weakest point in the funnel.
- [ ] Set a Q2 (days 91–180) target: number of active retainer clients, number of one-off engagements per month

## Milestone targets (illustrative — adjust to founder's financial needs)

| Milestone | Target by day 90 |
|---|---|
| Discovery calls held | 8–12 |
| Proposals sent | 5–8 |
| Paid engagements delivered | 2–4 |
| Referral partner relationships started | 3–5 |
| LinkedIn posts published | 10–12 |
| Testimonials collected | 1–2 |

## What "good" looks like at day 90

- At least one fully delivered, high-quality engagement with a happy client willing to give a testimonial or referral
- A validated (not just estimated) sense of real pricing tolerance in the market
- A short, real pipeline of warm leads rather than a cold start
- Confidence in the actual time-per-engagement, informing whether pricing and capacity assumptions need adjusting

## Assumptions / needs founder input

- Assumes founder can dedicate meaningful time (minimum ~15–20 hrs/week) to business development in the first 90 days alongside delivery work.
- Milestone targets are illustrative placeholders — replace with numbers tied to the founder's actual financial runway and income target.
