# Business Overview

## What Pharos Security is

A founder-led, independent cyber security advisory practice serving New Zealand small and medium businesses. The business provides risk assessment, practical security uplift planning, staff awareness training, incident readiness preparation, and ongoing advisory support, delivered in plain English, sized for SMEs, and free of product sales or vendor bias.

## What Pharos Security is not

- Not a managed security service provider (no 24/7 monitoring, no SOC)
- Not a penetration testing / offensive security firm
- Not a digital forensics or incident response (technical remediation) firm
- Not an MSP or day-to-day IT support provider
- Not a reseller of security software or hardware

See [risk-boundaries.md](risk-boundaries.md) and [../03_Services/service-boundaries.md](../03_Services/service-boundaries.md) for the full detail on scope limits. Note: this list is under active review, see [../09-project-management/decisions-required.md](../09-project-management/decisions-required.md) question 1, which considers whether Pharos should add active technical testing services (vulnerability assessment, external attack surface review) once insurance and certification are confirmed.

## Business model

A hybrid of:
1. **Fixed-scope engagements**: the Cyber Risk Review, Microsoft 365 Basics Review, workshops, and starter packs, each sold as a defined deliverable for a quoted price.
2. **Project-based uplift work**: the SME Security Uplift Plan, typically following a Risk Review.
3. **Recurring advisory retainer**: Ongoing Cyber Advisor Support, sold as a monthly retainer for continued access to independent advice.

This mix gives the business predictable entry points for new clients (low-commitment first engagements) and a path to recurring revenue (retainers) without requiring the operational overhead of 24/7 service delivery.
