# Pricing Strategy

**Status: validated against published NZ/AU market data (see [Market validation](#market-validation) below) as of July 2026. Figures below have been checked against real competitor and market pricing signals, but no NZ competitor publishes exact fixed-fee pricing for a direct like-for-like comparison — treat these as evidence-informed, not confirmed-by-quote. Continue to sanity-check against real proposals and win/loss feedback in the first 90 days.**

## Pricing philosophy

1. **Price the outcome, not the hour** wherever possible — fixed-scope pricing is easier for SME buyers to say yes to than open-ended hourly billing, and it protects the advisor from scope creep dragging down effective hourly rate.
2. **Use ranges publicly, fixed quotes privately** — the website and marketing materials should show indicative ranges; the actual quote is confirmed after a scoping/discovery call.
3. **Keep the entry point genuinely low-commitment** — the Cyber Risk Review and Microsoft 365 Basics Review should be priced to make "yes" easy, since they're the primary lead-to-client conversion point.
4. **Build toward recurring revenue** — one-off engagements are valuable, but the Ongoing Cyber Advisor Support retainer is what makes the business sustainable and less dependent on constant new-client acquisition.

## What drives price within each range

- Business size (headcount, number of systems/locations)
- Complexity of existing environment (number of cloud platforms, degree of custom/legacy systems)
- Urgency/timeline (rush delivery may carry a premium)
- Travel requirement for in-person delivery outside the founder's base region

## Retainer tiers (Ongoing Cyber Advisor Support) — starting structure

| Tier | Suggested monthly fee (NZD) | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| Essentials | $500 – $700 | Monthly email/phone check-in, ad hoc advice (email response within 2 business days), annual mini risk review |
| Standard | $800 – $1,050 | Above, plus quarterly review call, priority response (1 business day), light involvement in vendor/tool decisions |
| Priority | $1,150 – $1,500 | Above, plus more responsive turnaround, more hands-on involvement in ongoing uplift plan execution, discounted rate on additional project work |

Even the top of this range sits well under the $1,500–$3,000/month floor typically seen for a genuine (if limited-scope) vCISO engagement in NZ — that gap is deliberate positioning, not underpricing: Pharos Security's retainer is ad hoc advisory access, not board-level security leadership. If demand consistently pushes against the Priority tier ceiling, that's a signal there's room to introduce a fourth "vCISO-lite" tier above it, not a signal the existing tiers are wrong.

## Discounting policy

- Avoid discounting the headline price; instead, adjust scope (e.g. shorter review, fewer staff sessions) to fit a tighter budget.
- Bundle discounts are acceptable and encouraged (e.g. Risk Review + Uplift Plan bundled at a modest discount to the sum of both) — this rewards commitment without devaluing the core price.
- Avoid "first client" or "founding client" discounts becoming a permanent anchor — if used to build initial case studies, cap it to the first 3–5 clients and say so explicitly in the internal pricing notes (not necessarily to the client).

## Payment terms (suggested starting point)

- Fixed-scope engagements: 50% deposit on proposal acceptance, 50% on delivery of final report/plan
- Retainers: monthly in advance, no lock-in contract initially (builds trust; revisit once demand is proven)
- Standard payment terms: 7 days from invoice for project balances

### Effort-rate sanity check

Using the effort estimates already documented in each [../03_Services/](../03_Services/) file, the fixed fees above imply the following effective hourly rates:

| Service | Estimated hours | Fee range | Implied effective rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cyber Risk Review | 7.5–10.5 | $1,800–$4,200 | ~$170–$400/hr |
| SME Security Uplift Plan | 10–13 | $2,000–$5,000 | ~$155–$385/hr |
| Microsoft 365 Basics Review | 5.25–7.25 | $800–$1,800 | ~$110–$250/hr |
| Email and Phishing Readiness | 5.25–6.75 | $600–$1,500 | ~$90–$220/hr |

All of these sit comfortably above the $47–$67/hr employee-equivalent market rate, which is expected and appropriate — an independent consultant's fee needs to cover business development time, admin, insurance, and non-billable hours that an employee's hourly rate doesn't have to. If real delivery time consistently runs longer than estimated, revisit the fee rather than silently absorbing the gap.
