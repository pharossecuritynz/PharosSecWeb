# Pharos Security — Brand Brief

A one-page snapshot of the brand. Each section is expanded in its own file in this folder.

## Brand essence

**Clear direction through uncertain waters.**

Pharos Security exists to make cyber security understandable and actionable for New Zealand SMEs. The name comes from *Pharos*, the ancient lighthouse of Alexandria — a fixed point that let sailors navigate safely at night. That's the role this business plays for clients: a steady, independent point of reference in a subject that otherwise feels overwhelming, technical, and fear-driven.

## Positioning in one line

Independent, plain-English cyber security advisory for New Zealand SMEs — helping owners understand risk, prioritise improvements, and prepare for incidents, without jargon or scare tactics.

See [positioning.md](positioning.md) for the full positioning statement and supporting pillars.

## Audience

New Zealand small and medium business owners and operations leads who don't have in-house security expertise, may already use an outsourced IT provider or MSP, and want independent advice they can actually act on. Full detail in [../02_Business_Strategy/target-market.md](../02_Business_Strategy/target-market.md).

## Personality

Calm. Practical. Direct. Quietly confident. Never alarmist, never salesy, never condescending. Think trusted advisor, not vendor. See [tone-of-voice.md](tone-of-voice.md).

## Visual direction

Deep navy backgrounds, white content sections, restrained teal/cyan accents, abstract beacon/light-beam motif, subtle coastal influence. Premium boutique consultancy feel — never a hacker/SOC/military aesthetic. Full detail in [visual-identity.md](visual-identity.md), [colour-palette.md](colour-palette.md), and [typography.md](typography.md).

## Primary tagline

**Clear guidance. Safer business.**

Alternatives and usage guidance in [tagline-options.md](tagline-options.md).

## Brand promise

We will always give you advice that's honest, proportionate, and written for how your business actually operates — never advice sized for an enterprise you're not, and never advice designed to sell you more than you need.

## What this brand is not

- Not an enterprise SOC or managed security service provider
- Not a penetration testing or offensive security shop
- Not a fear-based sales operation
- Not a reseller pushing a particular security product
- Not trying to sound bigger or more corporate than a founder-led practice actually is
