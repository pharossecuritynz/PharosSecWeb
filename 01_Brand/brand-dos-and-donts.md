# Brand Do's and Don'ts

A quick-reference gut check for any new content, design, or client-facing material.

## Messaging

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Lead with what a risk means for the business | Lead with technical mechanism or acronyms |
| Use ranges and proportion ("this is worth prioritising") | Use absolutes ("this is critical," applied to everything) |
| Say "reduce risk" | Say "eliminate risk" or "guarantee protection" |
| Acknowledge what the client already does well | Open by listing everything wrong |
| Recommend what's achievable in the client's next 90 days | Recommend a full enterprise framework regardless of fit |
| Say clearly when something is out of scope and refer out | Stretch scope to avoid turning away revenue |
| Use real NZ business context and examples | Use generic, US-centric, or enterprise case studies |

## Tone

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Sound like a calm, capable advisor | Sound like a vendor closing a sale |
| Use plain English, define terms on first use | Assume the reader knows security terminology |
| Be warm and direct | Be casual/slangy or stiff/corporate |
| Use "you" and speak to the business owner | Write in passive, impersonal voice |

## Visual

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Use deep navy, white, mist, teal, cyan, charcoal | Introduce off-palette colours without deliberate extension of the system |
| Use abstract light/beam motifs | Use padlocks, shields-with-skulls, or hacker imagery |
| Use restrained, geometric line icons | Use stock "cyber security" clip art (binary code, glowing locks) |
| Keep teal as an accent | Let teal dominate a layout |
| Use generous whitespace | Cram content to fit more on a page |
| Keep motion slow and subtle | Use urgent, blinking, or sharp animation |

## Sales behaviour

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Offer a free, low-pressure discovery call | Use hard-close sales tactics |
| Quote a realistic range and explain what drives cost | Lowball to win the job then scope-creep |
| Say "I don't think you need that yet" when true | Upsell services the client doesn't need |
| Follow up once or twice, then respect silence | Chase repeatedly after two follow-ups with no response |

## Naming and claims — hard boundaries

Never claim, imply, or allow marketing copy to suggest:
- 24/7 monitoring or a security operations centre
- Penetration testing or offensive security testing capability
- Forensic incident response or digital forensics capability
- Compliance certification issuance (e.g. "we'll certify you as ISO 27001 compliant")
- Guaranteed prevention of breaches or incidents

See [../03_Services/service-boundaries.md](../03_Services/service-boundaries.md) for the full list of what Pharos Security does and does not do.

## Quick test before publishing anything

Ask: *"Would a calm, experienced, independent advisor actually say this — or does it sound like a security vendor trying to close a deal?"* If it's the latter, rewrite it.
