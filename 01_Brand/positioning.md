# Positioning

## Positioning statement

For New Zealand small and medium business owners who need to understand and reduce their cyber risk but don't have in-house security expertise, **Pharos Security** is the independent cyber security advisory that translates cyber risk into clear, practical action. Unlike enterprise security consultancies, managed security providers, or generic IT-provider add-ons, we work exclusively with SMEs, speak in plain English, and never sell products or push fear-based urgency.

## Core positioning (long form)

Pharos Security provides practical, independent, plain-English cyber security advice for New Zealand SMEs. We help business owners understand risk, prioritise improvements, work better with their existing IT provider, prepare for incidents, and reduce everyday cyber risk.

## The three positioning pillars

### 1. Independent
We don't resell security products, hold vendor partnerships that bias recommendations, or take commissions. Our advice is not shaped by what we can sell — only by what genuinely reduces risk for the client.

### 2. Right-sized
We work exclusively with SMEs. Every recommendation is calibrated to what a business with limited budget, no dedicated IT security staff, and finite time can realistically do. We don't import enterprise frameworks wholesale.

### 3. Calm and clear
We explain cyber risk the way a good GP explains a health issue — plainly, honestly, without inflating the threat to create urgency. Clients should leave every interaction with more clarity and less anxiety than they started with.

## Where we sit relative to alternatives

| Alternative | How Pharos Security differs |
|---|---|
| **General IT provider / MSP** | MSPs manage day-to-day IT and often bundle "security" as a checkbox add-on. We provide the independent risk assessment and prioritisation an MSP typically isn't positioned (or incentivised) to give — and we work *with* the MSP to help them implement what matters. |
| **Big-4 / enterprise security consultancies** | Built for large organisations with dedicated budgets and security teams. Their frameworks, reports, and pricing don't translate to a 15-person business. We exist specifically for the segment they don't serve well. |
| **Penetration testing firms** | Pentesting answers "can someone break in a specific way right now?" — a narrow, technical, often premature question for an SME that hasn't yet got basic controls in place. We focus on the broader risk picture and practical uplift first. |
| **Security product vendors (e.g. email security tools sold direct)** | Vendors are incentivised to sell their product. We have no product to sell, so our recommendation is the same whether or not it results in a purchase. |
| **DIY / ignoring it** | Many SMEs currently do nothing beyond what their IT provider bundles in, because cyber security advice feels inaccessible or irrelevant to their size. We make it accessible. |

## What we want to own in the client's mind

*"The person I call when I need an honest, independent answer to 'are we doing enough, and what should we do next?'"*

## Category

Independent cyber security advisory (not: managed security service provider, not: penetration testing firm, not: IT support/MSP).
