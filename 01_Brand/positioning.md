# Positioning

**Status note (2026-08-22):** revised under the Pharos v2 directive to lead with independent security assurance rather than a list of services. See `00-business/decisions.md` for the full reasoning. Superseded wording is not deleted from git history but is no longer live.

## Positioning statement

For New Zealand small and medium business owners who have IT support but no independent way to know whether their security is actually good enough, **Pharos Security** is the independent adviser who checks, explains, and prioritises, without selling technology or replacing the IT provider already in place.

## Core proposition (one sentence)

Pharos Security helps small New Zealand businesses understand whether sensible security controls are actually in place, and what to do next.

## The relationship we own

Your IT provider manages your technology. Pharos helps you know whether your security is good enough.

This is the sentence that should do the most work on the website and in conversation. It answers the question every prospect is quietly asking ("don't I already pay someone for this?") before they have to ask it.

## What Pharos actually sells

Not scans. Not reports for their own sake. Not a technology stack. Pharos sells:

- independent security assurance
- clarity about where a business actually stands
- prioritisation, so effort goes to what matters most
- practical, achievable guidance
- verification, not assumption
- an informed second opinion on what the IT provider or a vendor is proposing
- ongoing oversight where a business wants it

Assessments, reviews, and reports are how that value gets delivered. They are not the product itself.

## The three positioning pillars

### 1. Independent

Pharos does not sell software, does not sell managed IT services, and does not earn commissions from recommended products. Recommendations are shaped only by what the engagement finds, not by what there is to sell. This is the single strongest trust signal available to a security adviser and should be stated plainly, not implied.

### 2. Right-sized

Pharos works exclusively with businesses roughly 5 to 50 staff. Every recommendation is calibrated to what a business with limited budget, no dedicated security staff, and finite time can realistically do. Enterprise frameworks are a source, not a script.

### 3. Calm and evidence-based

Findings are presented the way a good second opinion works: plainly, honestly, without inflating the threat to create urgency, and always clear about what was actually checked versus what was reported. See the evidence, interpretation, action standard in `04_Operating_Manual/pharos-security-baseline.md`.

## Where we sit relative to alternatives

| Alternative | How Pharos differs |
|---|---|
| **MSP / IT provider** | Manages and supports the technology day to day. Pharos independently reviews the security outcome of that work and helps the business understand risk and priorities. Not a replacement, and not an audit designed to catch the MSP out. |
| **Enterprise security consultancies** | Built for organisations with dedicated budgets and security teams. Their frameworks, reports, and pricing don't translate to a 15-person business. Pharos exists specifically for the segment they don't serve well. |
| **Penetration testing firms** | Answers a narrow technical question ("can someone break in this specific way right now?") that's often premature for a business that hasn't yet confirmed its basic controls are in place. Pharos focuses on the broader risk picture first, and refers to a specialist pentest firm when that's genuinely the next right step. |
| **Security product vendors** | Incentivised to sell their product. Pharos has no product to sell, so the same recommendation stands whether or not it results in a purchase. |
| **Doing nothing** | The real default competitor. Most SMEs currently rely on whatever their IT provider bundles in, because independent security advice has felt inaccessible or irrelevant to their size. |

## What we want to own in the client's mind

*"The people who can independently tell us whether our security is actually good enough, and what to do about it, without trying to sell us something."*

## Category

Independent cyber security adviser for New Zealand small businesses. Deliberately not: MSP, MSSP, SOC, penetration testing company, compliance consultancy, privacy law firm, or SaaS vendor.

## The MSP relationship, stated plainly

Pharos is not hostile to MSPs and does not build messaging around catching them out. Most MSPs are doing a reasonable job with the scope they've been given; the gap is usually that nobody has independently checked the security outcome of that work, not that the MSP is failing.

The working principle: **the MSP operates and supports the technology; Pharos independently reviews the security outcome and helps the business understand risk and priorities.** Good MSPs are a referral channel, not a competitor. See `IT_PROVIDER_SECURITY_ASSURANCE` in `03_Services/it-provider-security-assurance.md`.

## Independence statement (for website and methodology use)

> Pharos Security is independent. We do not earn commissions from recommended security products, and we do not sell managed IT services. Recommendations are based only on what an engagement finds.
